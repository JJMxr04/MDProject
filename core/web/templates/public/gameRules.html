Full Game Rules Explanation

Tournament Structure

Each tournament round consists of a single match.

Matches are played between two teams and comprise 11 games.

One of these 11 games is designated as the Golden Game, which carries extra importance and is worth 2 points. This game is chosen and uploaded by Paradise Sports.

Match Structure

Each match includes 11 individual games, each based on events with predetermined market outcomes.

Golden Game: This pivotal game holds double value and must be predicted by both teams.

Scoring System

Teams score points by correctly predicting event outcomes.

For every correct prediction, 1 point is awarded.

The Golden Game is worth 2 points, offering a strategic edge.

The team with the most points after 11 games wins the match. If the points are tied, tiebreakers are used to determine the winner.

Offensive and Defensive Picks

Teams alternate between offensive and defensive roles:

  • Offensive Role: The team uploads an event for the opponent to predict.
  • Defensive Role: The opposing team predicts the outcome of the uploaded event.

Each team submits 5 offensive events labeled A through E.

Hidden Picks: In 4 out of 5 uploads, the defensive team does not know the offensive team's selection.

Revealed Pick: One event must be revealed by the offensive team, allowing the defensive team to see and strategize accordingly.

Gameplay Details

Submission Time: Offensive uploads must be submitted at least 6 hours before the game time. The defensive team has until the start of the game to submit their prediction.

Points Disclosure: Points are revealed once the event concludes, and the winning team is awarded points based on the result.

Example of Gameplay:

  • Initial Event: Team A uploads the event "Bills +2.5 vs Bengals" as an offensive pick.
  • Hidden Game: The pick is hidden from Team B, which must make its prediction without knowing Team A's choice.
  • Outcome: The game ends, and points are awarded based on the event's result. For instance, if the Bengals win by 6 points, Team B scores a point.

Detailed Offensive/Defensive Process:

  • Offensive Upload: Team A uploads an event outcome by submitting it to the website.
  • Defense Informed: Paradise Sports notifies Team B of the event through a notification system. Team B then makes its defensive pick by submitting it to the website.
  • Revealed Event Strategy: When Team A chooses to reveal their pick (e.g., "Men’s CBB, USC -3.5 vs KSt"), Team B can see this and decide whether to align with the pick or go against it for strategic reasons.

Golden Game Rules

Paradise Sports uploads the Golden Game, which is worth 2 points.

Both teams must submit their picks for this game before game time.

Teams must also submit their predicted total score for this game, which serves as the first tiebreaker if needed.

Tiebreakers

First Tiebreaker: The total points predicted for the Golden Game are used to break ties.

Second Tiebreaker: Each team submits one extra game that cannot be chosen by the opponent.
