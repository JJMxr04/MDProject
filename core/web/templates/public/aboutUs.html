About Paradise Sports

Paradise Sports is a competitive prediction community for people who love the strategy of sports betting without the financial risk. We give bettors the data, the tools, and the head-to-head format to prove their edge against other sharp minds — and have fun doing it.

Our story

Paradise Sports (“PBL”) started out of a simple observation: the most rewarding part of sports betting isn't the money. It's being right when others were wrong, spotting value the books missed, and watching a Sunday-afternoon prediction pay off because you saw something the line-makers didn't. The risk and the rush are baked into the same bet, and most people end up grinding through the losses for the occasional dopamine hit of a big win.

We wanted to keep the parts of betting that make it interesting — the analysis, the competition, the community discussion — and strip out everything else. No bookmaker accounts to fund. No deposits or withdrawals. No chasing losses. Just picks, leaderboards, and bragging rights you actually earned. The result is a prediction-game platform that feels like betting at its best: sharp, social, and skill-based.

What we are

A community-driven sports prediction platform. Members compete head-to-head and in tournaments by picking outcomes across the major US and international leagues. We grade picks against real results from a transparent data feed, and we display per-bookmaker odds so members can see how their picks line up against the market.

What we are not

A sportsbook. We do not accept wagers, hold balances, process payouts, or facilitate real-money gambling in any form. Paradise Sports is a prediction game for entertainment and skill ranking. If you're looking to place real-money bets, this isn't the right service.

Our mission

Build the most honest competitive-prediction community on the internet. That means three things to us:

Transparency

Every pick is graded against the same public data feed. The catalog of supported sports, leagues, books, and market types is visible to members — you always know what you can pick and how it's scored.

Community first

Leaderboards, head-to-head matches, and tournaments are the product. We're built for bettors who enjoy the social side as much as the analysis.

No predatory mechanics

No dark patterns, no “just one more pick” streak bonuses, no affiliate funnels to real sportsbooks. We make money from advertising on public marketing pages — the product itself stays clean.

How the platform works

Members sign up free, browse upcoming events from every active sport and league we cover, and submit picks before game time. Each pick goes against another member (in a head- to-head match), against the whole community (in a public game), or against a tournament bracket. When the event finalizes, our system grades every pick automatically — no manual moderation, no disputes, just deterministic settlement against the public score data.

Behind the scenes we run our own data aggregator that pulls events, markets, selections, and per-bookmaker quotes from multiple upstream providers. The aggregator normalizes the data, handles the lifecycle (event scheduled → live → finished → settled), and surfaces everything through a clean API the portal reads from. This is the same shape of pipeline professional traders use — we just expose it to bettors.

For the full picture of supported sports, leagues, books, and market types, members can check the “What's available” page inside the portal at any time. The list is generated live from the aggregator so it's always accurate.

The team

We're a small, focused team based in the United States, building Paradise Sports out of a shared love of the strategy side of sports. Between us we've spent years building data pipelines, web platforms, and online communities — experience we're applying to make prediction-game tooling that respects its users.

We don't have outside investors. We don't take sportsbook referral money. We're funded by display advertising on our public pages and (eventually) optional premium features for power users. That funding model keeps our incentives aligned with the community: a better, fairer game is what keeps members coming back.
