Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026 · Effective: May 12, 2026

Paradise Sports (“PBL,” “we,” “us”) operates mdproject-production.up.railway.app and related services (the “Site”). This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and what choices you have. We’ve tried to keep it in plain English — if anything is unclear, email us.

1. Information we collect

We collect three categories:

Information you give us

  • Account data: username, email, password (hashed), first/last name, optional avatar, optional friend code.
  • Waitlist signup: email address.
  • Communications: if you email us, the contents of that email and your address.

Information collected automatically

  • Log data: IP address, browser type/version, OS, referring/exit pages, timestamps. Used for debugging, abuse prevention, and aggregate analytics.
  • Device & usage data: session identifiers, pages viewed, clicks, time on page.
  • Cookies: see §3.

2. How we use information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To run the betting-game features you opt into (matches, tournaments, brackets, leaderboards).
  • To send transactional email (verification, password reset, tournament reminders, match results).
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Cookies & similar technologies

We use only strictly-necessary cookies and local storage — session, CSRF protection, and login state. These cannot be turned off because the Site cannot function without them.

We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party tracking. We do not run Google Analytics, Google AdSense, Facebook Pixel, or any similar third-party tracker on this Site. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated and a consent banner added before any tracking cookies are introduced.

You can clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Third-party services we use

  • Postmark (or equivalent SMTP provider) — transactional email delivery.
  • Railway / Coolify — hosting infrastructure.
  • Stripe — payment processing (only if you make a purchase). Stripe’s privacy policy applies to payment data: stripe.com/privacy.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Service providers listed in §4, under written contract, for the purposes described in this policy.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, when legally compelled.
  • A successor in interest (acquirer, merger partner), with notice to you.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account; then 30 days for backups, then permanent deletion.
  • Email logs: 90 days, then deleted.
  • Server access logs: 30 days, then aggregated or deleted.
  • Backups: rolling 35-day window.

7. Your rights

If you’re in the EU, UK, or Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Access: request a copy of your data.
  • Rectification: correct inaccuracies.
  • Erasure: request deletion.
  • Restriction: limit how we process your data.
  • Portability: receive your data in a portable format.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

If you’re in California (CCPA / CPRA)

  • Right to know what we collect, use, share.
  • Right to delete personal information.
  • Right to correct inaccurate information.
  • Right to opt out of “sale” or “sharing” of personal information (we do not sell or share your personal information).
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

To exercise any right, contact us. We will respond within 30 days (45 in some jurisdictions).

8. Cookie controls

We don’t use tracking or analytics cookies, so there’s no consent banner to manage. The strictly-necessary cookies (session, CSRF, login) cannot be disabled because the Site cannot function without them — clearing them will log you out.

9. Children’s privacy

The Site is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We’ll update this page when our practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top of the page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will also be announced on the homepage or via email.

11. Contacting us

Questions or requests? Email privacy@paradisesports.example or use the contact page.
