Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Paradise Sports: Media and Entertainment (“Paradise Sports,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) operates a free, friendly sports-prediction game (the “Service”). The Service is intended for adults 18 years of age or older and does not involve real-money wagering. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the Service.

Information you provide: username, email address, first and last name, date of birth (used to verify you meet our 18+ age requirement), password (stored hashed), and optional profile details such as a short bio and avatar image. We also collect content you submit, such as game picks, friend connections, and support requests.

Information collected automatically: IP address, browser type and version, device and operating-system information, referring website, and the pages or features you interact with — collected through server logs, cookies, and our self-hosted analytics (see Section 4).

2. Cookies and similar technologies

We currently use only functional cookies needed to keep you logged in and to secure forms (session and CSRF cookies). Our analytics (see Section 4) runs in cookieless mode and sets no cookies. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising cookies. If this changes, we will update this Policy and provide any consent controls required in your region.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • operate and maintain the Service;
  • create and authenticate your account;
  • run game features such as matches, tournaments, and friends;
  • respond to your support requests;
  • detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues;
  • improve the Service; and
  • comply with our legal obligations.

4. Analytics

We use Matomo, an open-source analytics platform that we self-host on our own infrastructure, to understand how the Service is used and to improve performance and features. Analytics data is never shared with, or sold to, any third party. Our Matomo instance is configured for privacy: it runs in cookieless mode (no analytics cookies), anonymizes IP addresses before storage, and honors your browser’s Do Not Track setting — if you enable Do Not Track, your visits are not recorded. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless service that measures aggregate page performance.

5. Age requirement and children’s privacy

The Service is for adults 18 and older. We ask for your date of birth at registration to verify this. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 18, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with:

  • service providers who help us run the Service — including Resend (email delivery), Stripe (payment processing), and our cloud storage and hosting providers — strictly to perform services for us (our analytics is self-hosted, so analytics data never leaves our infrastructure);
  • legal authorities when required by law; and
  • a successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Payment card details are handled by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.

7. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to opt out of certain processing. To make a request, contact us and we will respond as required by applicable law.

10. Third-party links

The Service may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
