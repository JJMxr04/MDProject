Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This privacy policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and usage on our website.

Data Collection

We collect and process only essential data as part of website functionality and for analytics purposes. For details on the type of data processed, refer to our Data Processing and Privacy Policy Guide.

Consent Management

We have integrated the consent management tool ConsentManager by ConsentManager AB (Håltgelvågen 1b, 72348 Västerås, Sweden, mail@consentmanager.net) to facilitate the collection of user consent for data processing and the use of cookies or similar technologies. This tool allows you to consent to or reject certain functionalities on our website, including external elements, streaming content, statistical analysis, measurement, and personalized advertising. You can adjust your consent settings at any time.

The purpose of integrating ConsentManager is to enable you to control your privacy settings on our website, while allowing us to meet legal obligations for documentation and compliance. ConsentManager processes personal data and information from your device, such as your IP address, and stores your settings as long as they remain active. Every two years, ConsentManager will prompt you to update your consent preferences, which will then be stored again for another two-year period.

The legal basis for this processing is Art. 6 Para. 1 S. 1 lit. c) and Art. 6 Para. 3 S. 1 lit. a) in conjunction with Art. 7 Para. 1 GDPR, and additionally lit. f) for our legitimate interest in managing consent records for compliance. You can object to this processing by sending an email to mail@consentmanager.net, especially if you have specific reasons based on your particular situation.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience, analyze site usage, and enable Google services like Analytics and Ads. Any data collected through cookies is anonymized to protect your privacy. You can control cookie preferences to determine what data you choose to share.

Google Analytics & Ads

To improve our services, we use Google Analytics to gather insights about site traffic and Google Ads to support our business. This data helps us understand user preferences while upholding privacy standards. For more on Google’s data policies, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell or share your personal information with third-party advertisers. All analytics and advertising data is anonymized and used solely to enhance site experience and service offerings.

Your Rights

You have the right to manage your cookie settings directly through your browser and through ConsentManager. If you wish to know more or exercise your data rights, please feel free to contact us at our support page.
