Match formats

A match is a head-to-head contest between you and one opponent across a set of games, plus a Golden Game. Pick a format when you create or invite:

  • Blitz — 2 games + Golden Game. Settles in ~2 days.
  • Classic — 3 games + Golden Game. Settles in ~4 days.
  • Marathon — 5 games + Golden Game. Settles in ~7 days.

Every game is built around a real sporting event with set market outcomes. The Golden Game is auto-selected by the system and is worth double.

How a match works

Players alternate offensive and defensive roles across the regular games:

  • Offence (slot owner): you pick first — choose the event, market and outcome.
  • Defence (opponent): you then pick within the same market the owner chose.

You own half the regular games and defend the other half. Plus the Golden Game, where the system locks the market and both players pick simultaneously (picking the same outcome is allowed — it's a valid blocking tactic).

Deadlines: the owner picks at least 8 hours before event start; the opponent picks any time before start. Miss a pick and that game is forfeited. Settlement is automatic — when each event finishes, every selection is graded (won, lost, push or void) and scores update.

Scoring

  • 1 point for a correct prediction on a regular game.
  • 2 points for a correct prediction on the Golden Game.
  • 0 points if you don't pick a game before its event starts (forfeit).

The player with the most points once all events settle wins the match.

The Golden Game

Every match has one Golden Game. Its event and market are auto-selected when the match is created, it's worth 2 points, and both players must submit a pick before the event starts.

You also submit a predicted total score for the Golden Game — this is mandatory and is the first tiebreaker.

No draws & tiebreakers

Matches always produce a winner — they can't end in a draw. If players are level on points, the result is decided by a deterministic cascade:

  1. Closest predicted total score on the Golden Game.
  2. Golden Game outcome correct vs incorrect.
  3. More correct regular picks.
  4. Earlier-locked picks (decisiveness).
  5. A final deterministic fallback so there is always exactly one winner.

Duels

A duel is the fastest way to play: one game, opposite sides. You pick a friend, an event and a market, then take one side — your opponent automatically gets the other. When the event settles, whoever called it right wins the duel.

Duels are friend-to-friend and settle as soon as the event ends. Unlike matches, a duel can end in a draw if the event is postponed or the market voids.

Pick of the Day

Each day the system features one game on your dashboard. Make one free pick before it locks. Correct picks climb the daily Pick of the Day leaderboard, and picking every day builds your streak.

Tournaments

Tournaments are brackets of matches. Each round is a head-to-head match against another player; win to advance to the next round. Climb the bracket to take the title — and season ranking points along the way.
