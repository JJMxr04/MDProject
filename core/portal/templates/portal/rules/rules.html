Game rules

How matches, golden games, and scoring work.

Tournament structure

Each tournament round consists of a single match. Matches are played between two teams and comprise 11 games.

One of these 11 games is designated as the Golden Game — it carries extra weight and is worth 2 points. The Golden Game is auto-selected by the system when the match is created.

Match structure

Each match includes 11 individual game slots, each based on a sports event with predetermined market outcomes.

Golden Game: the system pre-selects the market for this slot. Both players pick within that locked market — picking the same outcome is allowed (it's a defensive blocking tactic).

Scoring system

  • 1 point for a correct prediction on a regular slot.
  • 2 points for a correct prediction on the Golden Game.
  • 0 points if you don't pick a slot before its event starts (forfeit).

The team with the most points after all 11 events finalize wins the match. If tied, tiebreakers decide the winner.

Offensive & defensive picks

Teams alternate between offensive and defensive roles per slot:

  • Offensive role: the slot owner picks first — they choose an event + market + outcome.
  • Defensive role: the opponent then picks within the same market the owner chose.

Each team owns 5 regular slots; you're the opponent on the other 5. Plus 1 Golden Game where the system locks the market and both pick simultaneously.

Gameplay details

Owner pick deadline: at least 8 hours before event start.

Opponent pick deadline: any time before event start.

Settlement: once the event finishes, the system grades every selection (won, lost, push, or void) and updates your match score automatically.

Golden Game rules

The Golden Game's event + market are auto-selected from the events catalog when your match is created. Both teams must submit their picks before the event starts. The Golden Game is worth 2 points.

Each team also submits a predicted total score — the first tiebreaker if the match ends tied.

Tiebreakers

First tiebreaker: total score predicted for the Golden Game — closest to the actual final score wins.

Second tiebreaker: each team submits one extra game that the opponent can't choose.
